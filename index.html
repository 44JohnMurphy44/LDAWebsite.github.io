What We Build

End-to-end MCO infrastructure. Delivered fast.

From member eligibility to final claim payment — LDA engineers the infrastructure that connects every step. We move at the speed your operations require, without replacing the systems you've already invested in.

01 Data Infrastructure & Integration
One integration layer. Every system your MCO runs on.

MCOs typically operate across five to ten platforms with no shared data layer. LDA builds the integration infrastructure that connects them — pulling eligibility, prior auth, care management, claims, and provider data into a single queryable environment. We work with ETL pipelines, REST and SOAP APIs, HL7 ADT feeds, and FHIR interfaces, inside your existing stack. Staff stop reconciling across systems. Leadership stops asking which number is right.

02 Workflow Automation & Operations
Authorization to payment — with your rules encoded at every step.

LDA builds the operational layer your MCO runs on: prior auth routing with embedded clinical criteria and TAT enforcement, claims processing workflows wrapped around your adjudication engine, closed-loop referral management between care teams and providers, and auto-generated member correspondence at each decision point. Deadlines, payer rules, and regulatory requirements are built into the system — not left to staff memory.

03 Custom Development & Analytics
Custom database applications and reporting — where vendor tools stop.

LDA builds database applications from the schema up — designed for healthcare data complexity, CMS audit requirements, and real production load. We deliver HEDIS measure sets, CMS encounter data packages, state regulatory submissions, and operational dashboards that run directly from source systems. When your vendor's configuration limits are the bottleneck — authorization logic, integration scope, reporting depth — we build what the platform can't.
